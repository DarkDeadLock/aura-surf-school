ABOUT
AURA SURFOur Story · Our Passion · Weligama

BORN FROM
THE OCEAN

Aura Surf School was founded in 2016 by Kasun Perera — a lifelong surfer from Weligama who grew up riding the warm waves of the Indian Ocean.

We believe surfing is more than a sport. It's a connection with nature, a test of limits, and a freedom unlike anything else.

Today, Aura is home to 8 passionate, ISA-certified instructors ready to guide surfers from across the globe to their perfect wave.
